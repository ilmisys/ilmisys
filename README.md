## 📖 About

**ILMISYS** is a software company that builds practical tools for schools, students and the wider community.

We believe the software that schools and students use every day should be **simple, dependable and easy to adopt**. That is why we focus on education, and on tools that are genuinely useful.

### What's in the name?

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ILM** (علم) | The Arabic word for *knowledge* |
| **SYS** | *Systems*: the ways knowledge is organized and shared |

---

## 🎯 Mission

To make learning and school management easier by building software that saves time and reduces friction, so teachers, students and families can focus on learning.

## 👁️ Vision

To put well-designed, dependable education software within reach of every school and every student, not just a few.

---

## 💡 Our Values

- **Useful first**: Software should save time, not add work.
- **Simple to adopt**: Schools and students should be productive from day one.
- **Built with users**: Feedback from teachers, parents and students shapes what we build.
- **Made for the community**: Good tools should be within everyone's reach.

## 🧩 Our Products

### 🏫 [Ilmify](https://ilmisys.com/ilmify): School ERP

An ERP system that brings a school's daily operations together in one platform.

| Module | |
|---|---|
| 📝 Admissions | 🗓️ Attendance |
| 💰 Fees | 📊 Exams & Results |
| ⏰ Timetable | 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Communication |

👉 App: [ilmify.com.pk](https://ilmify.com.pk/)

### 🧠 [Evalumax](https://ilmisys.com/evalumax): Quiz & Exam Practice

A practice platform where students answer questions, get instant feedback and track their progress. Teachers and schools can also use it for practice outside the classroom.

👉 App: [evalumax.com](https://evalumax.com/)

### 🛠️ [Community Tools](https://ilmisys.com/community-tools): Free Utilities

Small, focused and free tools for schools, students and the wider community, such as **grade**, **GPA** and **attendance calculators**.
